Our Sacred Journey

SINCE 1992

How the school came into being

The author (Syed Yousuf Hasan Tahir) had long been yearning for an institution of good standard imparting instruction in both secular and spiritual sciences. He proposed this idea to his father (late Qari Sayyed Rasheed-ul-Hasan, Second Preacher, Jaamia' Masjid, New Town).

On the 27th 1992 of Ramadaan, the Night of Power, the Islamic Institute for Education came into being. We started our school with the prayers of al-qaari Sayyed Rasheed-ul-Hasan. The academic year began with only four boys on rolls.

Aims & Objectives

Global Outreach

To promote Islamic education all over the world, bridging the gap between sacred wisdom and modern knowledge.

Equal Opportunity

To provide opportunities of education for all, regardless of background, ensuring every soul has access to learning.

Academic Uplift

To work tirelessly for the educational uplift of our community through rigorous standards and spiritual Tarbiyah.

Healthy Competition

To arrange competitions in every field of education to foster excellence, confidence, and character in students.
